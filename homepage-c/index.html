About Us · 25-year legacy

25

years

Twenty-five years of nurturing young minds.

Tungal Schools, with campuses across North Karnataka in Jamkhandi, Hulyal, Bagalkot and Vijayapura, is committed to nurturing confident, well-rounded students. Each centre offers a calm, disciplined study environment, modern classrooms and caring faculty who guide students academically and personally.

Run by the Shri K K Tungal Memorial Trust (R), Jamkhandi, the institution has continued to educate thousands of students at an affordable cost.

Education must not only enrich and develop the individual but must help him to become a useful and productive member of society.From the Tungal educational philosophy
Shri K K Tungal Memorial Trust (R), Jamkhandi
